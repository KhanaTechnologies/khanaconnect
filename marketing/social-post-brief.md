# Khana Technologies — social post brief

## Who we are
Khana Technologies (Pty) Ltd, Pretoria, South Africa.
We are a managed partnership: we build the client's online store or booking page, host/run it, and stay. We are not a once-off web agency that disappears.

- Website: khanatechnologies.co.za
- WhatsApp / phone for contact: +27 76 635 6790
- Footer line on images: "We build it, launch it, and stay."
- Company email: contact@khanatechnologies.co.za

## Voice (Jason)
Write like a Pretoria business owner talking to another owner. Direct, short sentences, plain South African English. No hype, no "we would love the opportunity," no desperate CTAs. Show what happens for the guest and the owner. Do not invent discounts, half-price offers, or free months.

## Who the posts are for
Business owners, not their customers.

Best fits:
- Shops / makers who sell on WhatsApp and Instagram
- Salons, clinics, tutors, photographers who already have clients
- Restaurants / guesthouses that already have regulars
- Businesses whose website was built and then abandoned

Do not aim posts at people who only want a cheap DIY site (Wix/Shopify self-serve), or who depend on a marketplace for new customers (Dineplan diner app, Booksy/Fresha marketplace).

## What Khana actually does (only claim these)
- Online store or bookings (both only on higher plans — don't imply every client gets both by default)
- Booking/order page on the client's own site / domain
- Confirmation emails from the client's own mailbox (Gmail, Microsoft 365, cPanel mail, etc.)
- Booking/order details on the client's WhatsApp Business number
- WhatsApp booking reminders the customer can confirm or reschedule with one tap (added Nov 2026; works once the client's WhatsApp templates are approved)
- A WhatsApp to the owner's own phone for every new booking or order (optional)
- Guest/customer list stays with the business
- Local Facebook/Instagram ads can be set up; Meta bills the client's card
- We stay for support and updates

## What Khana does not do (never claim)
- Public diner directory / consumer booking app
- Reserve with Google
- Floor plans, table maps, walk-in queues
- SMS reminders
- "We will fill your restaurant with customers" / promised cover numbers
- Handing over a full source-code platform as a one-time sale with no monthly partnership
- Replacing Dineplan's diner network for restaurants that live on that traffic

## Visual style (Recraft / image AI)
- Square 1080×1080 for feed; 9:16 Stories = headline + one card only
- Pale cool off-white / slight blue-white background
- Small navy crescent logo, top
- Extra-bold navy headline, usually two short lines, centered
- Gray subtitle with middle dots (·) when used
- One white rounded app card, soft shadow, thin border, flat vector UI, line icons, lots of whitespace
- Footer: "We build it, launch it, and stay." + khanatechnologies.co.za
- Never on the image: buttons, "See live demo", prices, rand amounts, "Swipe", promo stickers, floating badges
- Avoid terracotta/cream AI-default looks, purple gradients, dark neon glow, emoji clutter

## Caption rules
- Captions may end with khanatechnologies.co.za
- Product/story posts: no prices
- Price posts only when explicitly asked for a "deal / plan" campaign — use real numbers only if supplied in that request
- No inventing features or competitor attacks
- Ask for a conversation / WhatsApp when there is a CTA, not "buy now"

## Proven post formats
**A) Restaurant carousel (5 slides, one story):** guest books ("Saturday. 8pm. Two guests.") → email from restaurant mailbox → WhatsApp with the same details → owner's tonight list → Reserve button on their site opens the calendar.

**B) Shop carousel (5 slides, same frame):** order placed ("One cake. Collect Saturday.") → email from shop mailbox → WhatsApp → owner's order list → shop on their own site.

**C) Single posts (not price cards):** the booking/order happens on your site · confirmation from your mailbox · details on your WhatsApp · one list for the owner · abandoned website → we stay after launch · WhatsApp selling is fine; a proper order page stops chaos.

**Carousel frame (reuse every time):** same logo, background, card size/position, and 5 progress dots (current = navy, others = light gray). Only the card content and headline change. Headlines should feel unfinished so people swipe ("Then the email arrives." / "And a WhatsApp.").

## Output per batch
Recraft/image prompt (style block once + one scene) · caption (ready to paste) · format (single / carousel / Story) · audience tag (restaurant / shop / salon-clinic / abandoned site). Product/story posts first; no price posts unless asked.

## Sample captions (tone)
Restaurant: "Saturday, 8pm, two guests. The confirmation comes from the restaurant's own mailbox. The details follow on WhatsApp. The owner opens one list and the name is already there. The guest booked from the restaurant's own site. khanatechnologies.co.za"

Shop: "One cake. Collect Saturday. The confirmation comes from the shop's own mailbox. The details follow on WhatsApp. The owner opens today's orders and it is already there. The customer ordered from the shop's own site. khanatechnologies.co.za"
