POSTER PROMPT — ECOMMERCE OFFERING (1080×1080 Facebook post)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Design a clean, professional Facebook poster for a South African technology company called Khana Technologies.

Subject: Ecommerce offering — one dashboard to run an online store, products, orders, and marketing tools.

Visual style:
- Modern SaaS marketing poster, not flashy or cheesy
- Light background (soft off-white or very light blue-grey)
- Accent colours: navy blue #1e4d8c, primary blue #2563eb, teal #0d9488
- Minimal, lots of breathing room — not cluttered
- Subtle rounded UI mockup or abstract dashboard shapes (cards, chart line, shopping bag icon) — no fake brand logos from other companies
- Professional, trustworthy, warm — aimed at small business owners in South Africa

Layout:
- Top: small space for Khana Technologies logo
- Headline area (large, bold, sans-serif): "Your online store. One dashboard."
- Subheadline (smaller): "Products · Orders · Payments · Marketing tools"
- Bottom third: short line "We build it, launch it, and stay." + website khanatechnologies.co.za
- Optional small CTA pill button style: "See live demo"

Typography feel: Plus Jakarta Sans or similar modern rounded sans-serif. Bold headline, regular body.

Do NOT include: stock photo people pointing at laptops, neon gradients, clip art, lorem ipsum, US dollars, generic "Shop Now" ecommerce clichés, overcrowded feature lists.

Mood: Calm confidence. Partner you can trust — not a cheap template.

Format: Square 1080×1080 px, safe margins for Facebook feed.

CONSISTENCY RULES — USE ON EVERY KHANA CANVA DESIGN
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BRAND NAME
  Primary: Khana Technologies
  Product: Khana Connect (use when showing the platform/dashboard)
  Never: KhanaConnect one word, never all caps KHANA

COLOURS (add to Canva Brand Kit)
  Navy:     #1e4d8c  — headlines, dark accents
  Blue:     #2563eb  — buttons, links, primary accent
  Teal:     #0d9488  — secondary accent, status highlights
  Purple:   #6366f1  — B2B / wholesale designs only
  Background: #f8fafc or white
  Text dark: #1e293b
  Text muted: #64748b

FONTS (Canva equivalents if Plus Jakarta not available)
  Headlines: Plus Jakarta Sans Bold / Montserrat Bold / Poppins SemiBold
  Body:      Plus Jakarta Sans Regular / Inter / Open Sans
  One headline font + one body font only — never mix more than 2 families

LOGO
  Always use your Khana logo file — top-left or centred top
  Clear space around logo = height of the "K" mark minimum

LAYOUT HABITS
  • One main idea per poster — not a feature dump
  • Headline max 6–8 words
  • Subline max one short sentence or 3–4 dot-separated items
  • Always include khanatechnologies.co.za (or /demo for demo CTAs)
  • CTA buttons: rounded pill or rounded rectangle, blue #2563eb fill, white text
  • Margins: keep text 48px+ from edges on 1080 designs

TONE OF COPY (human, not AI)
  ✓ "We build it, launch it, and stay."
  ✓ "One place for your shop, bookings, and orders."
  ✓ "No plugin puzzle."
  ✗ "Leverage synergistic solutions"
  ✗ "Empowering businesses to scale"
  ✗ Long em-dash lists
