2. WHO WE’RE TALKING TO
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Audience A — SMB owners (retail, food, services)
  • Running or growing a business in SA
  • Tired of plugin puzzles, scattered tools, agencies that disappear
  • Want: one dashboard for sales, bookings, and marketing tools

Audience B — Wholesale / B2B merchants
  • Sell to shops, distributors, or trade buyers
  • Need: tier pricing, buyer accounts, online ordering, multi-warehouse stock
  • Pain: phone/email orders, spreadsheets, no buyer self-service

9. DO / DON’T
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DO:
  • Use “partner” language, South African context (Rands, local examples)
  • Show real UI (demo screenshots) where possible
  • Keep text minimal on image — detail in caption
  • Include logo + khanatechnologies.co.za

DON’T:
  • Promise “custom app in 48 hours” or unrealistic timelines
  • Promise guaranteed revenue outcomes (e.g. “we will make you more money”)
  • Look like a generic offshore dev shop
  • Overcrowd with feature lists — one idea per ad
  • Use Lorem ipsum — use Cape Fresh / sample stats above
