# Batch 3: what happens after launch (5 posts)

Follows `social-post-brief.md`. No prices on the images. Never name a competitor or knock one; the posts just show what we do and let people compare.

**The angle:** almost every competitor ad in the feed (Nov 2026) says the same thing: a laptop mockup, "Website from R___", "Request a quote". They sell the build. We sell what the site does every day after it goes live: bookings and orders on your own site, WhatsApp confirmations and reminders, a ping to your phone, and a team that stays. So none of our posts show a laptop, and none lead with a price.

**How to use:**
- Paste the style block, then the post's scene, as one prompt.
- Recraft settings: style "Vector illustration" or "Digital illustration". Use Square for feed posts and 9:16 for the Story.
- Before posting, read every word on the image. If anything is misspelled, regenerate. If it still won't come out right, use `templates/slide.html`.
- Suggested order: one post every 2 to 3 days, 1 → 5.
